Two users, two real browsers.

Kane CLI drives Manvith and Sudeep from plain-English steps, each in their own Chrome on their own port of the proxy. Separate cookies, separate sessions, and Dead Heat always knows whose request is whose.

lane 4801lane 4802

Both saves, frozen at the line.

When a save reaches the proxy it is frozen. The first user waits until the second user’s save arrives too. Reads pass straight through. In the real Kanboard race, Sudeep’s save waited for Manvith’s.

Released at one instant.

Both saves reach your app together, or one after the other in either order. In all three cases both users had already read the old state. Kanboard answered both saves within of the release.

Kept, refused, or silently lost.

Kane CLI reloads each browser and checks, in your own words, that this user’s work is still there. Kanboard 1.2.58 answered 200 to both saves and showed no warning. After a reload, Manvith’s new description was gone.
